# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Deck skins: selectable playing-card skins with low and high contrast faces (Customize Deck).
- Rebinds: animated art and floating soul layers (including The Soul) for single objects.
- Default shaders on specific cards (visual only).

## 1.0.1

- Playing card faces and the shop sign can now be reskinned as whole sheets.
- Mod author set to 12problems.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Loads textures (PNG), sounds and music (OGG) and text (JSON) from a reskin pack folder outside the mod, through Malverk.
- Vanilla sheets: jokers, tarots, planets, spectrals, vouchers, boosters, enhancers, decks, seals, tags, blinds and stakes.
- Custom sheets: art for objects added by other mods.
